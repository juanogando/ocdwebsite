The read. Flipsnack publishes the finished catalogue. Pacdora builds the box and its dieline. Abacus rents the models. OCD•LOOKS is the only one that takes your own product from spec drawing to campaign, and the only one that puts it on a model.
Also in the frame. Pacdora is the packaging half of what OCD•LOOKS does for product and fashion. The apparel equivalents are CLO3D, Browzwear (freelancer $75 / mo, enterprise roughly $200–500 / seat / mo) and Style3D ($35–299 / mo, enterprise from about $10K / yr). They model garments and write tech packs. None of them render campaigns or put a locked cast on a model.
