## A · ORDER FORM — the commercial commitment

Two shapes. **Pick one per tier.** Shape 1 is simpler to run and is what Clerk can
bill today; shape 2 is the standard enterprise instrument and needs an invoice.

### Shape 1 — annual, paid in advance

> **Term.** $4,389 a seat covers a 12-month Initial Term, beginning on the
> Subscription Start Date.
>
> **Renewal.** The subscription renews for successive 12-month terms at the
> then-current list price unless either party gives written notice at least 30 days
> before the end of the current term.
>
> **Cancellation.** You may cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end
> of the term you have paid for. Fees already paid are not refunded in whole or in
> part, and the subscription is not suspended or paused mid-term.

### Shape 2 — annual commitment, billed monthly

> **Term.** $399 a seat a month covers a 12-month Minimum Term, beginning on the
> Subscription Start Date. The monthly amount is the payment schedule; the 12-month
> Minimum Term is the commitment.
>
> **Renewal.** At the end of the Minimum Term the subscription continues month to
> month at the then-current list price, cancellable with 30 days' written notice,
> unless a new term is agreed.
>
> **Early termination.** $399 a seat for each month remaining in the Minimum Term
> becomes payable if you terminate before the Minimum Term ends, other than for our
> material breach under §B.4. Access continues until the end of the month in which
> termination takes effect.

### ENTERPRISE additions

> **Term.** $4,500 a month covers 10 seats over a 12-month Initial Term. $450 a month
> covers each additional seat. A 24-month Initial Term is available by agreement.
>
> **Renewal and notice.** The Initial Term renews for successive 12-month terms unless
> either party gives written notice at least 60 days before the end of the current
> term.
>
> **Seats added mid-term.** $450 a month covers each seat added during a term. Added
> seats run co-terminously with the current term and are charged from the date of
> addition to the end of that term. Seat counts may be increased at any time and are
> reduced only at renewal.

## B · TERMS OF SERVICE — the sections to publish

Written to sit in the existing `/terms` page. Plain English on purpose: a term clause
that reads like a trap gets negotiated out or disputed.

### B.1 Term and renewal

> Your subscription begins on the date your first payment is accepted and continues
> for the Initial Term shown on your plan at checkout or on your order form. Unless
> you or we give notice, it renews automatically for further terms of the same length
> at the price then listed.
>
> To stop a renewal, cancel in the app under **Manage account → Billing**, or write to
> looks@ogandocreativedesign.com, at least 30 days before your current term ends
> (60 days on ENTERPRISE). We will email you a reminder before each renewal, showing
> the renewal date, the amount and how to cancel.

### B.2 Fees and payment

> $0 is refundable once a term has begun, except where the law requires otherwise or
> where we have materially breached these terms. Prices are per seat unless stated
> otherwise and exclude tax.
>
> Where your plan is billed monthly against a Minimum Term, each monthly payment is an
> instalment of that term, not a month-to-month arrangement.

### B.3 What happens if you cancel or do not pay

> If you cancel, your access continues until the end of the term you have paid for.
>
> If a payment fails, we will tell you and give you 10 days to fix it. After that we
> may suspend access. Suspension does not shorten your term or reduce what is owed.
>
> If you end a Minimum Term early, the remaining instalments for that term become
> payable at once. Nothing else is added — there is no separate penalty or fee.

### B.4 If we are the ones at fault

> You may end your subscription immediately, and we will refund the unused part of any
> prepaid term, if we materially breach these terms and do not fix it within 30 days of
> your written notice.

*(Keep this clause. A minimum term with no exit for the vendor's own failure is the
clause that gets struck, and offering it costs almost nothing.)*

### B.5 Your licence to what you make — the clause that matters

> **While your subscription is active,** you may use anything you create in OCD Rapid
> Studio for any lawful purpose, including commercial use, on the plans that include
> commercial rights.
>
> **Work you have published stays yours, permanently.** Any image, video, spin,
> lookbook or sheet you have published, placed in market, or delivered to a customer,
> manufacturer, retailer or agency during a paid term remains licensed to you for that
> use after your subscription ends. You never have to take down or stop using work you
> put out while you were a subscriber.
>
> **Work you have not yet published needs an active subscription.** Output you have
> generated and downloaded but not yet published is not licensed for commercial use
> once your subscription ends. To publish it later, resubscribe.
>
> **Brand models and casts.** A model you create with Cast-a-Model may be used in work
> you publish during a paid term, under the same rule above. The cast itself, and the
> ability to generate further images from it, ends with the subscription.

### B.6 Credits

> Plan credits are issued for each billing period and expire at the end of that
> period. They do not roll over and are not refundable, exchangeable or transferable.
> Credits bought as top-ups are valid for 12 months from purchase. Cancelling ends
> any unused plan credits at the end of the paid period.

### B.7 Confidentiality and restrictions — ENTERPRISE only

> Where your agreement includes API access, the KIKO Command Station, or a build
> running inside your own network, each party will keep the other's confidential
> information confidential, use it only to perform the agreement, and protect it with
> at least the care it uses for its own confidential information. This obligation runs
> for the term and for 5 years afterwards; for anything that qualifies as a trade
> secret, it runs for as long as it stays a trade secret.
>
> You will not copy, decompile, reverse-engineer or attempt to derive the methods,
> models, prompts, pipelines or source of the service, permit anyone else to do so, or
> use the service to build a competing product. Access is licensed to named
> individuals and may not be shared.
>
> Materials we deliver to you may carry identifying marks unique to your account.

## C · WHAT THE PLAN PAGE MUST SAY

Best practice, and what California's auto-renewal law expects: the commitment is
disclosed **before** payment, in the same visual field as the price, not buried in the
terms.

> **LICENSE · $4,389 a seat a year**
> 12-month minimum term. Renews yearly unless you cancel 30 days before renewal.
> Cancel any time — access runs to the end of the term you have paid for. No refunds
> on a term already begun.
> Work you publish while subscribed stays licensed to you permanently.

And at checkout, a separate tick, unticked by default:

> ☐ I understand this plan has a 12-month minimum term and renews automatically.

Then a confirmation email that repeats the term length, the renewal date, the amount
and the cancellation route. Keep a record of the tick against the account — it is the
only thing that proves disclosure if a charge is disputed.
